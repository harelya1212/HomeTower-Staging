# HomeTower - Product Description

## Name
HomeTower

## What it is
HomeTower is a family-collaborative app that gives a household one clear, continuously updated picture of its financial future. The household uploads its financial documents once - pay stubs, tax returns, retirement and college-savings statements, brokerage reports - and the app turns them into a live tax projection, a monthly snapshot, and a forward forecast, all measured against goals the family sets itself.

## The final product
- Document-first intake: upload any financial file; the household profile fills itself from the documents and the user only confirms or corrects. Typing is the last resort.
- Live tax projection: federal + state, updated all year, tracked against a refund target the household chooses - not a one-time calculator in January.
- Monthly snapshot ("Where do we stand?"): one screen per topic, one big number, red/yellow/green status, and the top 3 actions that matter this month.
- Sale simulator (TaxImpact): before selling any investment, see the full tax cost of selling now vs waiting - capital gains, loss carryforwards, wash sale, NIIT, state tax - inside the same live projection.
- Family goals at the centre: retirement per spouse, college funding per child, major purchases - each goal with a forecast line from today forward.
- Accuracy is stated, never hidden: every number carries a level, X (directional) to X+++ (filing grade), and the app says plainly what it does not know yet.
- Private by design: local-first, no selling of data, no third-party trackers inside the app. Free for users; revenue from contextual ads only.

## Target audience
US households with two earners, children, and more than one financial moving part - salaries, 401(k) and HSA plans, 529 college savings, a brokerage account, a mortgage - who today get their financial picture once a year from a tax preparer and guess the rest of the year. Primary user: the household's money manager, typically 30-55, not a finance professional, who wants one simple answer: are we on track, and what should we do this month?

## One line
TurboTax tells you what happened last year. HomeTower tells your family where it stands today - and what to do next.
