## North Star
The goal is not only to reach retirement with "enough" money. The goal is to gradually move the family from income based on work and concentration in one employer to a structure of diversified assets - 401(k), Roth, HSA, taxable investments, real estate and Social Security - that can replace the salaries without a material drop in standard of living. Every equity event is a Capital Allocation decision: Sell / Hold / Wait / Diversify / Fund Goal / Harvest Tax, not only Buy / Sell.
